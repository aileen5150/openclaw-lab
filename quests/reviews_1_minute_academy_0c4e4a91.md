# Reviews 1 Minute Academy

**Review: 1Minute Academy**

I've been using 1Minute Academy for about three months now, primarily to fill gaps during my commute and lunch breaks. The core concept is straightforward: bite-sized lessons designed to be completed in roughly one minute, covering business, tech, and personal development topics.

**What Works**

The microlearning format genuinely fits into real life. I completed 47 lessons last month without feeling overwhelmed. Topics range from project management fundamentals to quick Excel tips, and each one cuts straight to practical application—no padding. The mobile experience is smooth, which matters when you're juggling a busy schedule. I also appreciate that lessons don't require heavy background knowledge; they're accessible to someone pivoting into a new field.

**What's Missing**

The depth is limited by design. You won't get comprehensive courses here—this isn't a replacement for Coursera or LinkedIn Learning. Some topics feel surface-level, and I wished for deeper dives on subjects I found genuinely interesting. Additionally, the platform lacks discussion forums or community features, so learning remains solitary.

**Verdict**

1Minute Academy excels as a supplementary learning tool for busy professionals who want to build micro-habits around continuous learning. It's ideal for executives, managers, or anyone whose schedule makes traditional courses impractical. However, if you're seeking certification-level content or structured curriculum, look elsewhere.

For what it is—a quick-hit knowledge booster—I recommend it. It's become a daily habit for me, and that's saying something given how many apps I download and never open.
